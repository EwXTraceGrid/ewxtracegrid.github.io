WHY AIRBORNE?

Traditional ground-based RF detectors (e.g. from Saab or Rohde & Schwarz) are:

  • ❌Too heavy and bulky
  • ❌Must be deployed near the frontline
  • ❌Easily disabled by enemy fire
  • ❌Cost $60k–$500k+ per unit

EwX:TraceGrid integrates the direction finder directly onto a drone. If a drone is shot down — we simply replace it. The network remains intact.

PASSIVE DETECTION

Even fiber-controlled drones without radio transmitters can be detected. They reflect ambient electromagnetic waves — captured via passive radiolocation.

Effective detection range: ~2.3 km (⁴√30 km) — enabling tactical awareness in close-range scenarios.

COMPOSABLE

Can integrate with other networks, apps, or defense systems. Open API. Community-extensible. No vendor lock-in.

GLOBAL

Anyone can deploy a node — no central server required. Truly permissionless infrastructure for worldwide deployment.

AUDITABLE

Raw data can be encrypted while key events remain on-chain and verifiable. Private but transparent where it matters.
