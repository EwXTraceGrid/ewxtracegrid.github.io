01 · INVESTMENT THESIS

Low-cost drones changed the economics of the threat.

Persistent RF awareness is still often delivered through costly, centralized assets. That leaves a deployment gap: buyers need more sensing points, more flexible placement and a common picture across a protected area.

EwX approaches the problem as infrastructure. The node is the entry point; the network, software and accumulated measurement data are the compounding asset.

PASSIVE

Listen without revealing the sensor

The RF receiver observes emissions without transmitting a detection signal.

DISTRIBUTED

Add nodes instead of one monolith

Coverage and location confidence can improve as sensing points are added.

SOFTWARE-LED

Turn measurements into recurring value

Classification, maps, alerts, integration and data quality sit above the hardware.

02 · SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

From radio energy to a decision-ready map.

The architecture separates sensing, signal processing and delivery. That allows the RF hardware to evolve while the operational software and dataset remain valuable.

01

Scan

Observe defined spectrum windows and detect signals above a disciplined threshold.

02

Measure

03

Fuse

04

Deliver

03 · EVIDENCE & VALIDATION

A real dataset—and a clear statement of what it proves.

On 7 July 2026, a two-channel laboratory sample was exercised against real urban ambient spectrum. The goal was to verify the automated workflow and local bearing stability before controlled ground-truth testing.

CLAIM BOUNDARY

Repeatability: yes. Absolute field accuracy: next.

The 0.61° figure is local repeatability on the best stable signal. It is not yet independent proof of absolute bearing error, drone-specific classification, operational range or multi-node location.
