The problem

Low-cost drones changed the threat. RF sensing must become distributed.

Defence units and critical infrastructure need persistent awareness of drone-related RF activity. Existing approaches can be expensive, centralized or difficult to scale. EwX is testing a network-first alternative built from synchronized, software-defined sensing nodes.

The product thesis

The durable value is not one detector. It is a shared RF awareness layer: sensing nodes collect disciplined measurements, software turns them into bearings and confidence scores, and multiple nodes can ultimately localize emitters across a protected area.

The architecture can support defence, border security and critical-infrastructure protection while preserving a clear path from laboratory evidence to operational validation.

What EwX is building toward

  • Passive RF observation without dependence on a single radar asset
  • Direction-finding evidence that can be inspected and reproduced
  • Multi-node fusion for emitter geolocation
  • A scalable software and data layer above proprietary sensing hardware

Next milestone

Turn repeatability into ground-truthed, multi-node evidence.

The next program is deliberately bounded and falsifiable. It is designed to answer the questions an investor, industrial partner or procurement team should ask before a larger deployment.

Controlled ground truth

Use known emitter positions and headings to measure absolute bearing error, stability and calibration drift.

4–6-node pilot

Deploy synchronized sensing nodes and test reproducible multi-node fusion under documented conditions.

Decision-grade dataset

Produce a traceable validation package covering accuracy, range, repeatability, failure modes and product requirements.
